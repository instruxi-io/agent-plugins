`enforcer dispatch watch` keeps one dispatcher running per graph that the work lobby (`GET /work`) advertises to the caller and restarts it when demand returns after it went idle, woken by `GET /work/stream` and a 30 s poll, with a doubling backoff for dispatchers that die young and a `watch:` line in `enforcer dispatch status`; a systemd user unit example is in `docs/graph/dispatch-watch.service.example`. `enforcer dispatch <graph> --for me|mine` launches only nodes assigned to the caller (the frontier's `for` filter).
