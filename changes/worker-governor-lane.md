- Dispatched workers with their own credential keep their governor receipts in a lane of their own (`~/.config/enforcer/governor/agents/<id>`, one per agent account per machine) and ship them under that credential only; the dispatcher ships the lane's tail before revoking a minted token. Before, the machine's single receipt outbox was shipped by whichever process ran next, under its credential, so one dispatched run showed up as two sessions under two accounts (decisions under the operator, telemetry under the agent).
