- A browser sign-in no longer ends in "signed out: HTTP 400 invalid_grant" minutes after `/enforcer:login` on a machine running several sessions. The OAuth refresh (POST and write-back of the single-use rotated pair) now runs in a detached child (`src/refresh-child.mjs`), so a hook killed at its timeout, a hook process exiting after its deadline race, or a killed headers helper can no longer lose the rotated pair after the server committed it; the next presentation of the rotated-out token is what made enforcer-v3 revoke the whole family. A refused refresh no longer replays the same token, a failed refresh never stamps or restores a pair that a sign-in, switch or logout saved meanwhile, and `login`, `logout`, `api-key` and `workspace switch` save under the refresh lock (the switch also records the new family's `refresh_expires_at`). `ENFORCER_REFRESH_INPROCESS=1` keeps the refresh in the calling process.
